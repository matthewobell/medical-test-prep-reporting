How to read these numbers

What counts as an ad click?

A click means someone tapped the ad’s sponsor link. Several taps on the same displayed ad count as one click. It does not tell us whether they purchased anything.

Does “Continued learning” mean someone watched the entire video?

No. It means someone tapped Keep Learning to proceed. Video watch time and completed views are not measured here.

Why are dates labeled UTC?

The current reporting system groups activity by UTC days. A UTC day may start on the previous afternoon in California. This preview uses October 28, 2026 as its sample “today.”
